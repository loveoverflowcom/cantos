## Changes and exception policy

Production resource architecture, locale preference adapters, auth/API/routing and native playback boundaries remain unchanged. The existing sandbox resource owner now owns two formerly screen-local diagnostic enums. Shared preview tool copy is fixed English, not a claim of bilingual preview UI; `PreviewSettings.locale` keeps selecting synthetic fixture content exactly as before. Generated inventory descriptions, names/state/token IDs are shown verbatim for inspection. Explicit Vietnamese fixture labels remain fixed-language samples for font/overflow/semantics testing; full Text fixture paragraphs retain exact content with vi-VN annotations. String-only accessible-name APIs do not establish native screen-reader voice negotiation. No new localization dependency was added.

Verification and final-source limitations are recorded in [the audit index](README.md).
